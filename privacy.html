Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

FitFairy lets you see yourself wearing outfits from posts and screenshots you share with the app. To do that it has to handle a photo of you, which is about as personal as data gets. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, where it goes, how long we keep it, and how you delete it. When we say "we" or "FitFairy" we mean the operator of the FitFairy app, reachable at support@fitfairy.net.

The short version

  • Your body photo lives on your device. We keep one private copy on our servers so outfits you share can be generated while the app is closed. It is deleted when you replace it, delete it, or delete your account, and automatically if you go about 90 days without generating anything.
  • Outfit images, generated try-ons, and Fit Check videos are stored privately, only for you, and are deleted when you delete the fit (fully purged within 30 days).
  • We never store the source video from a post you share, only its link and thumbnail.
  • We use AI providers to generate images and videos. Your photo is sent to them for processing and is not used to train their models under our agreements with them.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not run ads or advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, any time.

1. What we collect and why

Your body photo

During setup you take or choose a full-body photo. It is the reference for every try-on: the AI uses it to render you in the outfit. The photo is stored on your device as the primary copy. We also keep exactly one private copy per account on our servers (see section 3), because the "share from TikTok and it's ready when you open the app" feature has to run while the app is closed, and it cannot do that without access to your photo. That server copy also lets us restore your photo to a new device when you sign in with Apple, so you don't have to retake it. This means access to your photo is protected by the security of your Apple ID.

Outfit images you share or upload

When you share a post link, we fetch that post's public thumbnail. When you share or upload a screenshot, we use the screenshot. Either way the outfit image is stored privately with the resulting fit so the app can show it on your card. A screenshot captures your whole screen, so it may incidentally include other content on your device; you choose what to share.

The link to the source post

We store the URL of the post you shared so you can tap back to it and so the original creator stays credited. We do not download, store, or process the video itself.

Generated try-ons and Fit Check videos

The images and short videos the AI produces are stored privately in your account and cached on your device. They are yours to save, share, or delete.

Account information

The app creates an anonymous account for you on first launch, identified by a random ID, no email required. If you choose Sign in with Apple (for example to start a free trial or to keep your fits across devices), Apple gives us a stable identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email or a private relay address. We also keep an Apple sign-in token for the lifetime of your account, used only to revoke our access to your Apple ID when you delete your account, as Apple requires.

Purchases

Subscriptions and add-on packs are bought through Apple. We do not see your card number. We receive purchase records (product, price, currency, country, and store transaction identifiers) via our subscription provider so we can unlock what you paid for and keep your balances correct.

Usage information

We record basic events about how the app is used (for example: app opened, setup completed, a fit was added and from which kind of source, a result was viewed or saved, a Fit Check was started). These are stored in our own database under your account ID and are used to understand what's working and to fix problems. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Device and diagnostic information

If you tap Report a Problem, the app pre-fills an email with your app version and device model so support can help faster; you see and control everything before it is sent. If you enable notifications, we store a push token so we can tell you when a try-on is ready.

Clipboard

When you open the Add Fit screen, the app may check your clipboard on your device for a TikTok link so it can offer to use it. Links from other platforms are recognised only to tell you they aren't supported. Nothing from your clipboard is stored unless you confirm.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate try-on images and Fit Check videos you request.
  • To run background generation for posts you share while the app is closed.
  • To keep your fits, balances, and subscription state in sync with your account.
  • To notify you when a result is ready (only if you opt in).
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and keep the service secure.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the app.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your photos or results for advertising, for marketing to others, or to train AI models.

5. Deleting your data

Delete a fit: removes its outfit image, try-on, and video from your device immediately and from our servers within 30 days.

Replace your body photo: Profile → Update your photo. The old copy is deleted from your device and our servers at the same time. To remove your photo entirely, delete your account.

Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, every fit, your body photo, your balances, and your usage data from our servers, revokes our Sign in with Apple access, and clears the app's data on your device. It cannot be undone.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple; cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or we will be unable to stop the renewal for you.

6. Security

All traffic uses TLS. Files are stored in private buckets, encrypted at rest, and served only through short-lived signed links scoped to your account. Database access is restricted by row-level security so one user can never read another's rows. AI provider keys live only on our servers, never in the app. Because the AI models need to see your photo to generate a result, we cannot offer end-to-end encryption; "encrypted at rest" here means platform-level encryption on our provider's storage.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Most of these you can exercise directly in the app (viewing your fits, deleting fits, deleting your account). For anything else, email support@fitfairy.net and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Children

FitFairy is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with a photo or other personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top and let you know in the app before the change takes effect. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.
