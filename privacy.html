Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

FitFairy lets you see yourself wearing outfits from posts and screenshots you share with the app. To do that it has to handle a photo of you, which is about as personal as data gets. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, where it goes, how long we keep it, and how you delete it. When we say "we" or "FitFairy" we mean the operator of the FitFairy app, reachable at support@fitfairy.net.

The short version

  • Your body photo lives on your device. We keep one private copy on our servers so outfits you share can be generated while the app is closed. It is deleted when you replace it, delete it, or delete your account, and automatically if you go about 90 days without generating anything. Screen replays we use to fix problems can also show it (see section 1).
  • Outfit images and generated try-ons are stored privately, only for you, and are deleted when you delete the fit (fully purged within 30 days).
  • Clothing you save to Mix is backed up privately to your account so it comes back if you reinstall the app or move to a new device, and is deleted from our servers when you delete it.
  • Find Similar sends the clothing photo you choose to search to a search provider, SerpApi, which runs the search on Google. It never sends your body photo or your try-ons. Your searches are stored privately in your account until you delete them.
  • We never store the source video from a post you share, only its link and thumbnail.
  • We use a third-party AI provider, Google, to generate try-on images (it receives your body photo and the outfit) and to recognize the clothing in photos you search. What we send is processed only to give you the result and is not used to train its models under our agreement with it.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not run ads or advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, any time.

1. What we collect and why

Your body photo

During setup you take or choose a full-body photo. It is the reference for every try-on: the AI uses it to render you in the outfit. The photo is stored on your device as the primary copy. We also keep exactly one private copy per account on our servers (see section 3), because the "share a post and it's ready when you open the app" feature has to run while the app is closed, and it cannot do that without access to your photo. That server copy also lets us restore your photo to a new device when you sign in with Apple, so you don't have to retake it. This means access to your photo is protected by the security of your Apple ID.

Outfit images you share or upload

When you share or paste a link (a post, a product page, any web page), the app opens that page on your device and picks out its main public image; our servers never visit the link. When you share or upload a screenshot or image, we use that image. Either way the outfit image is stored privately with the resulting fit so the app can show it on your card. A screenshot captures your whole screen, so it may incidentally include other content on your device; you choose what to share.

Clothing you save to Mix

Mix lets you save clothing images (from a share, a link, or an upload) and combine them into an outfit to try on. Each saved piece is a resized copy of the image, the clothing type you picked, whether you favorited it, and the link it came from, if any. These are stored on your device and backed up privately to your account so your Mix comes back after a reinstall or on a new device when you sign in. When you try on a Mix outfit, its pieces are sent to the AI provider the same way an outfit image is, and the fit keeps its own copy of them.

Find Similar searches

Find Similar looks for clothing like a piece in a photo, with prices from online stores. You start a search from an image you share or upload, from a fit's original outfit image, or from a Mix piece. It never uses your body photo or a try-on. To run a search, we send the photo to our AI provider to identify the clothing in it, then send the photo and a short text description of the piece (for example "beige knit hoodie") to SerpApi, which runs Google Lens and Google Shopping searches. The AI provider also compares the public product photos in the results with your photo to drop poor matches. We store the searched photo, the clothing found in it, the results (product names, prices, store names, and links), which results you favorite, and the link the photo came from, if any, privately in your account, so you can reopen a search without running it again.

The link to the source post

We store the URL of the page you shared (with tracking tags removed) so you can tap back to it and so the original creator stays credited. We do not download, store, or process the video itself.

Generated try-ons

The images the AI produces are stored privately in your account and cached on your device. They are yours to save, share, or delete. Which fits you've marked as favorites is stored with them, so your favorites come back if you reinstall the app.

Collections

If you group fits into collections, we store each collection's name and which of your fits are in it, privately in your account, so your collections come back if you reinstall the app or sign in on a new phone.

Account information

The app creates an anonymous account for you on first launch, identified by a random ID, no email required. If you choose Sign in with Apple (for example to start a free trial or to keep your fits across devices), Apple gives us a stable identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email or a private relay address. We also keep an Apple sign-in token for the lifetime of your account, used only to revoke our access to your Apple ID when you delete your account, as Apple requires.

Purchases

Subscriptions and add-on packs are bought through Apple. We do not see your card number. We receive purchase records (product, price, currency, country, and store transaction identifiers) via our subscription provider so we can unlock what you paid for and keep your balances correct.

Usage information

We record basic events about how the app is used (for example: app opened, setup completed, a fit was added and from which kind of source, a result was viewed or saved, and for a link you share, the website's name (for example zara.com, never the full address), whether we found an outfit image on it, and how long that took), plus your answers to onboarding questions such as what styles you shop for and how you heard about FitFairy. These are stored in our own database under your account ID and are used to understand what's working and to fix problems. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. (We do use Sentry for crash and error reporting, described below.)

Device and diagnostic information

When the app crashes, hits an error, or runs slowly, it sends a diagnostic report to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry, so we can find and fix the problem. A report includes the technical error details, your device model and iOS version, the app version, how long screens took to load, the screens you visited just before the problem, and your random account ID so we can tell whether one person or many are affected. Sentry also records an approximate location (city and country) derived from your internet connection. Reports do not include your name, email, or IP address. For some sessions, including those where an error occurred, Sentry also records a low-detail replay of the app's screens so we can see what went wrong the way you saw it. Replays show the screen as it appeared, which can include your body photo, outfit images, and try-ons. Only the FitFairy team can view them, and they are deleted automatically within 90 days (see section 4).

If you tap Report a Problem, the app pre-fills an email with your app version and device model so support can help faster; you see and control everything before it is sent. If you enable notifications, we store a push token so we can tell you when a try-on is ready.

Clipboard

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste from clipboard on the Add Fit screen, and only to find a link in it. Nothing else from your clipboard is used or stored.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate the try-on images you request.
  • To run the Find Similar searches you request.
  • To run background generation for posts you share while the app is closed.
  • To keep your fits, balances, and subscription state in sync with your account.
  • To notify you when a result is ready (only if you opt in).
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and keep the service secure.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the app.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your photos or results for advertising, for marketing to others, or to train AI models.

5. Deleting your data

Delete a fit: removes its outfit image and try-on from your device immediately and from our servers within 30 days.

Delete a search: in Finds, removes its photo and results from your device and our servers.

Replace your body photo: Profile → Update your photo. The old copy is deleted from your device and our servers at the same time. To remove your photo entirely, delete your account.

Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, every fit, your collections, your Mix pieces, your Find Similar searches, your body photo, your balances, and your usage data from our servers, revokes our Sign in with Apple access, and clears the app's data on your device. It cannot be undone. Crash reports and screen replays already sent to Sentry are not deleted immediately; they are deleted automatically within 90 days (see section 4). Email us if you want them removed sooner.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple; cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or we will be unable to stop the renewal for you.

6. Security

All traffic uses TLS. Files are stored in private buckets, encrypted at rest, and served only through short-lived signed links scoped to your account. Database access is restricted by row-level security so one user can never read another's rows. AI provider keys live only on our servers, never in the app. Because the AI models need to see your photo to generate a result, we cannot offer end-to-end encryption; "encrypted at rest" here means platform-level encryption on our provider's storage.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Most of these you can exercise directly in the app (viewing your fits, deleting fits, deleting your account). For anything else, email support@fitfairy.net and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Children

FitFairy is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with a photo or other personal information, email us and we will delete it.
